# Minimalist Editorial Design System

## Overview
Guidelines for creating high-end, editorial, minimalist e-commerce layouts inspired by luxury architectural and gadget magazines. Emphasizes typography, negative space, and disciplined layout rhythm.

## Visual Hierarchy & Principles
1. **Less But Better**:
   - Every UI element must earn its place. Eliminate decorative borders, heavy drop shadows, colored gradients, and badge clutter.
   - Generous whitespace (`gap: 40px`, `64px`, `96px` on desktop) gives products breathing room and a luxury feel.
2. **Editorial Typography**:
   - Modern geometric sans-serif (Inter, Geist, Helvetica Neue, Manrope).
   - Hero Headings: Huge scale (48px - 72px), tight letter-spacing (`-0.03em`), bold, impactful.
   - Section Headings: Medium/large (28px - 36px), uppercase or clean title case.
   - Body & Captions: Neutral dark gray (`#333333`) or medium gray (`#777777`), 14px - 16px, 1.5 - 1.6 line-height.
   - Navigation: Clean, small (13px - 14px), slight letter spacing (`0.05em`).
3. **Structured Editorial Layout**:
   - Use strong grid systems (12-column desktop grid).
   - Contrast between wide full-bleed imagery and structured padded containers.
   - Product-first focus: Keep UI elements subdued so the product photos stand out crisply.
