# E-commerce Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

## Overview
Tactics and UI patterns to maximize conversion rates on product cards, category pages, and the checkout funnel while maintaining a clean, premium aesthetic.

## CRO Best Practices
1. **Primary Call to Action (CTA)**:
   - Make primary action buttons (`SHOP NOW`, `ADD TO CART`, `CHECKOUT`) visually dominant with solid black background, bold white uppercase text, and clear hover feedback.
   - Secondary actions (`EXPLORE`, `VIEW DETAILS`, `WISHLIST`) should use transparent backgrounds with clean hairline borders.
2. **Trust & Reassurance Elements**:
   - Prominently feature the 4 Core Trust Badges on the homepage and below product purchase buttons:
     - **Fast Delivery**: Quick & safe delivery dispatch.
     - **Easy Returns**: Simple, stress-free return policy.
     - **Quality Assured**: Hand-picked, tested tech and lifestyle products.
     - **Secure Payment**: 100% encrypted Shopify checkout.
3. **Product Card Clarity**:
   - Clearly display final price and compare-at/original price with strike-through (`₹1,999` vs `₹2,499`).
   - Show subtle percentage savings or "SAVE ₹500" badge without garish colors.
   - Include quick wishlist toggle (heart icon) and desktop quick-add action.
4. **Frictionless Checkout Journey**:
   - Direct button links into Shopify's native, secure checkout flow (`/checkout`).
   - Never introduce unnecessary intermediary registration steps before checkout.
